## Why this version is inbox-friendlier than the HTML one

- Plain text reads as personal correspondence, not bulk mail
- Single link, no buttons/images/shorteners
- No ALL CAPS, no "FREE", no manufactured urgency
- Conversational tone; asks for a reply (replies are the strongest
  positive signal to Gmail)
- Built-in whitelist PS trains the recipient's filter for future mails
